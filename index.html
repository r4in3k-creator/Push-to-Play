Install on this device

Add Push to Play to your home screen and it opens like an app — full screen, no browser chrome, and it works without a signal.

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari's toolbar.
  2. Scroll down and choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. It appears on your home screen like any other app.

Share your training

Copy a workout or a stretch of your training log as plain text — paste it straight into WhatsApp, Messages or anywhere else. Every share also carries an import code, so whoever gets it can load the whole thing into their own copy of the app instead of retyping it.

The short PTP2 line at the bottom is the import code. It's safe to leave in — the person reading gets a normal workout plan either way.
